The first is cloud building blocks, such as Microsoft Copilot Studio with Power Automate, Azure AI Foundry, Microsoft Entra Agent ID, Microsoft Purview and AWS Bedrock Agents. The second is ready-made business agents with governance built in, such as Salesforce Agentforce (for Salesforce customers) and STIV AI. The right choice depends on whether you want to assemble controls yourself or deploy governed agents directly into finance, legal, sales and operations.

## Why AI agent governance matters now in Singapore and APAC

Minister for Digital Development and Information Josephine Teo [announced the Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI](https://www.intelligentcio.com/apac/2026/02/17/singapore-launches-first-agentic-ai-governance-framework-as-armor-expands-asean-security-initiative/) at the World Economic Forum. According to the same report, the framework asks organisations to:

- **Maintain human accountability** for what AI agents do
- **Implement technical controls** over agent behaviour and access
- **Ensure operational transparency**, so that actions can be traced and explained

Those three expectations map closely to three product capabilities: approval gates (accountability), role-scoped access (technical control) and audit logs (transparency). For a practical walk-through, see our guide on [how to comply with IMDA's agentic AI framework](https://www.iamstivai.com/blog/how-to-comply-imda-agentic-ai-framework).

Most of what currently ranks for this topic is regulation news and announcements from security partners. Side-by-side product comparisons are rare. This article is meant to fill that gap.

## How we compared each AI governance platform for Singapore and APAC

We used five criteria, chosen because they correspond directly to the framework's expectations and to the questions buyers raise most often.

1. **Role-scoped access and agent identity.** Can you limit what each agent can see and do, and give it an identity that IT can manage?
2. **Human approval gates.** Can a named person approve consequential actions before they execute?
3. **Audit trails.** Is there a reviewable record of what the agent did, when it acted and who approved it?
4. **Integration without migration.** Does the platform work with your existing ERP, CRM and document systems, or does it require you to move data?
5. **Business-unit readiness vs developer tooling.** Is it a ready-made agent for finance, legal or sales, or a toolkit your engineers assemble?

We have not included data residency as a scored criterion because residency options vary by contract, region and configuration. Ask every vendor, including us, where prompts, outputs and logs are processed and stored for your deployment.

## Which platforms provide role-scoped access, approval gates and audit logs for AI agents?

A note on method: descriptions of third-party products reflect how each vendor positions the capability. They are not hands-on test results. Pricing is listed only where we could point you to the right place to confirm it.

### 1. Microsoft Copilot Studio + Power Automate

**Who it suits:** Organisations standardised on Microsoft 365 that want to build their own agents and workflows.

**Strengths:**
- Approvals can be built into agent flows, so a human signs off before certain steps run.
- Environment and data-loss-prevention policies give administrators a way to scope what agents can connect to.
- It sits close to the tools many employees already use.

**Limitations:**
- It is a build platform. Someone has to design, test and maintain each agent and its approval logic.
- Governance is spread across several admin surfaces, which often means it also relies on Entra and Purview (below).

**Pricing model:** Check Microsoft's official Copilot Studio and Power Automate pricing pages. Licensing varies by plan and usage.

### 2. Azure AI Foundry

**Who it suits:** Developer and data science teams building custom AI applications and agents on Azure.

**Strengths:**
- Positioned around content safety and tracing, which supports technical control and transparency.
- Flexible enough for bespoke agent architectures.

**Limitations:**
- It is aimed at engineers rather than finance, legal or commercial teams.
- Human approval steps and business-level audit views generally have to be designed by your team.

**Pricing model:** Described as usage-based. Confirm current rates on Azure's pricing pages.

### 3. Microsoft Entra Agent ID

**Who it suits:** IT and security teams that want to manage AI agents the way they manage users and applications.

**Strengths:**
- Focused on giving agents identities and applying access control. This addresses the role-scoped access criterion directly.
- Fits into existing identity governance processes for Microsoft-centric organisations.

**Limitations:**
- It is an identity layer, not an agent. It does not do finance or legal work by itself.
- Approval gates on business actions sit elsewhere in the stack.

**Pricing model:** Check Microsoft Entra licensing.

### 4. Microsoft Purview

**Who it suits:** Compliance, risk and data governance teams.

**Strengths:**
- Positioned for audit and data security posture across AI use, which supports the transparency expectation.
- Useful for organisations that already run Purview for data classification and retention.

**Limitations:**
- It governs and observes. It does not provide division-ready agents or approval workflows for business actions.
- It delivers most value when combined with the rest of the Microsoft stack.

**Pricing model:** Check Microsoft Purview licensing.

### 5. AWS Bedrock Agents

**Who it suits:** AWS-native developer teams building agents on foundation models.

**Strengths:**
- Offers a "return of control" pattern, where the agent hands an action back to the application, so a human or system can approve it before execution.
- Activity can be logged through AWS CloudTrail, which many security teams already monitor.
- Access can be scoped through AWS's identity and permissions model.

**Limitations:**
- It is developer infrastructure. Business users won't interact with it directly unless your team builds an interface.
- Approval workflows, reviewer routing and business-readable audit views are your team's responsibility.

**Pricing model:** Described as usage-based. Confirm on the AWS Bedrock pricing page.

### 6. Salesforce Agentforce

**Who it suits:** Organisations whose sales, service and customer data already live in Salesforce.

**Strengths:**
- Positioned with a trust layer and guardrails for agents operating on CRM data.
- Agents work close to the records and processes revenue and service teams already use.

**Limitations:**
- Its value is concentrated inside the Salesforce ecosystem. Finance close, contract review against a legal playbook, or cross-division executive work may need other tools.

**Pricing model:** Check salesforce.com for current Agentforce pricing.

### 7. STIV AI

**Who it suits:** CFOs, controllers, legal and commercial teams, revenue leaders and operations heads in mid-size and large enterprises who want governed AI agents working in their division without assembling a developer toolkit.

**What it is:** STIV AI is a Singapore-headquartered enterprise AI command center with division-specific agents for Executive, Sales, Marketing, Finance, Operations, Legal and Support.

**Strengths:**
- **Approval gates by design.** Every consequential action passes through human approval before it executes.
- **Full audit trails.** Each action and approval is recorded, which supports the framework's transparency expectation.
- **No migration.** Agents connect to existing systems rather than requiring data to move to a new platform.
- **Business-unit ready.** Agents are built for specific work, such as [finance reconciliation](https://www.iamstivai.com/blog/ai-finance-reconciliation-audit-trail), contract review against legal playbooks, and sales follow-up and lead qualification.
- **Flexible licensing.** Divisions can be licensed individually, as a full suite, or unified under one exclusive company-wide assistant through [STIV Unified](https://www.iamstivai.com/unified).

**Limitations:**
- It is not a general-purpose developer platform. Teams that want to build highly custom agents from raw model APIs may prefer Azure AI Foundry or Bedrock.
- It sits alongside your identity and compliance tooling rather than replacing it. Many enterprises will still run Entra, Purview or CloudTrail for organisation-wide controls.

**Pricing model:** Licensed per division, as a full suite, or as STIV Unified. See the pricing page or contact the team for details.

## Which AI governance platform fits your organisation?

A few plain rules of thumb:

- **You run on Microsoft and have a capable internal build team.** Combine Copilot Studio and Power Automate for agents and approvals, Entra Agent ID for identity, and Purview for audit. You get deep control, but you own the assembly.
- **You are AWS-native and building custom agents.** Bedrock Agents with return of control and CloudTrail gives engineers a solid foundation.
- **Your agent use cases live mostly in Salesforce.** Agentforce keeps agents close to your CRM data.
- **Division heads want governed agents working now.** If your CFO, General Counsel or CRO wants AI doing real work in existing systems, with a named human approving every consequential action and a record auditors can follow, a ready-made layer such as STIV AI is the more direct route.

These options are not mutually exclusive. A common pattern is to keep Microsoft or AWS for identity and organisation-wide logging, and to deploy division-specific agents on top.

### Questions to put to any vendor

1. Where are prompts, outputs and logs processed and stored for our deployment?
2. Can we define which actions require approval, and who approves them?
3. Can an auditor see who approved what, and when, without engineering help?
4. What has to move, if anything, for the agent to work with our ERP or CRM?
5. How does the platform support human accountability, technical controls and transparency under Singapore's agentic AI framework?

## Frequently asked questions

### Which AI agent platforms align with Singapore's Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI?

The framework expects human accountability, technical controls and operational transparency. Platforms that offer approval gates, role-scoped access and audit logs, such as Microsoft's stack, AWS Bedrock Agents and STIV AI, can support those goals. Alignment ultimately depends on how you configure and operate them.

### How do I add human approval to AI agent actions?

On developer platforms you build it yourself, for example with approvals in Power Automate flows or the return-of-control pattern in AWS Bedrock Agents. STIV AI routes every consequential action through a human approval gate by default.

### Will AI agent audit trails satisfy regulators such as MAS or PDPC?

No platform can guarantee regulatory acceptance on its own. Look for logs that show what the agent did, when it acted and who approved it, in a form your compliance team can review. Then confirm your approach with your own advisers.

### Can AI agents work with existing ERP or CRM systems without a migration?

Some can. STIV AI agents connect to existing systems without migration. Microsoft and AWS tools usually integrate through connectors or custom development, and Agentforce works best with data already in Salesforce.

### Is data stored in Singapore or APAC?

Residency depends on the vendor, region and contract, so ask every provider directly where prompts, outputs and logs are processed and stored for your deployment. Get the answer in writing before you sign.

### How is enterprise AI agent software priced?

Cloud building blocks such as Azure AI Foundry and Bedrock are generally usage-based. Others, such as Copilot Studio, Entra, Purview and Agentforce, are licensed through plans you should confirm on each vendor's pricing page. STIV AI is licensed per division, as a full suite, or as STIV Unified.
