## What is AI chief of staff software?

AI chief of staff software manages the operational and strategic coordination work around an executive: inbox triage, calendar management, briefing synthesis, commitment tracking and task routing ([Zamp](https://www.zamp.ai/blogs/ai-chief-of-staff)). In practice, products marketed under this label connect to your inbox, calendar and task systems and take on parts of that operational layer.

### AI chief of staff vs. executive assistant vs. human chief of staff

- **Executive assistant (human or AI):** manages the executive's time, travel, correspondence and logistics.
- **Human chief of staff:** drives priorities across the leadership team, holds people to commitments, and exercises judgement on what reaches the executive.
- **AI chief of staff:** automates the repeatable coordination work and, in more capable platforms, prepares or executes actions in business systems.

The distinction matters for large companies. An assistant that summarises meetings is useful. An agent that drafts a vendor payment, flags a contract clause or chases a pipeline opportunity is far more useful, and far more consequential. That is where governance stops being optional.

## How we compared the options

Most existing lists are written by vendors that rank their own product first, using scorecards they designed. We are a vendor too, so here are our criteria in plain view:

1. **Scope:** inbox and meetings only, or work across divisions such as finance, legal, sales and operations.
2. **Action:** whether the tool only informs, or can also take actions in business systems.
3. **Human control:** whether consequential actions pass through explicit approval gates.
4. **Accountability:** whether actions are recorded in audit trails a controller, auditor or regulator could review.
5. **Deployment and pricing:** how the tool connects to existing systems, and whether pricing is public, per seat or quote-based.

The ranking below reflects how well each option fits a **large company** on these criteria. A different set of priorities, such as lowest cost for a single executive, would produce a different order.

## Why governance matters for enterprise AI chief of staff software

None of the ranking pages we reviewed seriously discuss approval gates, audit trails or procurement. For an individual that is understandable. For a large company it is the main question.

### Approval gates

An approval gate pauses an AI agent before a consequential action, such as sending a client email, posting a journal entry or redlining a contract, and routes it to the accountable person. The agent does the preparation; a human makes the decision.

### Audit trails

An audit trail records what the agent proposed, what data it used, who approved it and when. This is what lets a controller close the books with confidence, or a general counsel explain a decision to a regulator.

### Connecting to existing systems without migration

Enterprises rarely want to move data into yet another platform. The practical alternative is agents that connect to the systems you already run, read and act within them, and leave the system of record where it is.
